Is Muse available in Morocco?

As of November 13, 2026, no. Muse is live in five countries: the US, since September 8, 2026; Canada, since September 18; Japan, since October 23; Singapore, since October 28; and Mexico, since November 5. Morocco is not among them, and no North African country is. The full country list shows the five launches, and nothing beyond them.

The Morocco row reads the same in two independent checks. An October 2, 2026 availability guide groups Morocco with Algeria, Tunisia, and Libya as not available with no date announced, while an availability table updated October 31, 2026 groups Morocco with Nigeria, South Africa, and Egypt under no launch announced. Both predate the Mexico launch, so read them for the Morocco status, not for the country total.

Meta has published no list of additional countries and no date for expansion, for Morocco or anywhere else. There is no official waitlist, no signup queue, and no country list you can check. The block message is the only status Morocco gets, and any site claiming a Morocco date is inventing one.

What does Morocco already have?

Moroccans have had a Meta assistant in their apps since early 2025. Meta's February 2025 Middle East and North Africa rollout made Meta AI fully available in Morocco from the start, in the same first group as the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Egypt, and Iraq, with Arabic support added at the same time, according to coverage of the announcement. It arrived inside the apps Moroccans already used daily, Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, and Messenger, through the blue circle icon, with no signup and no separate download.

French is the second language of the story. Meta AI's supported languages include French alongside Arabic, English, German, Hindi, Indonesian, Italian, Portuguese, Spanish, Tagalog, Thai, and Vietnamese, according to a Meta AI availability roundup. Morocco is a country where daily life runs in Arabic and French side by side, so an assistant that covers both languages fits how Moroccans actually read and write. That fit belongs to Meta AI today; whether Muse matches it is unconfirmed.

Do not confuse the two products. The blue circle already in your WhatsApp is Meta AI, the chat assistant. Muse is a separate agent that connects apps and acts on your behalf, and it has a separate country list that does not include Morocco. The difference between the two is the difference between an answer and an action, and the WhatsApp guide shows what the acting half looks like when it arrives.

Unconfirmed for both languages. Meta has published no language list for Muse, and no source checked confirms how well Muse handles Arabic or French in practice. Morocco is the hardest language case in this batch of guides, because daily Moroccan writing mixes Arabic, French, and Darija, the Moroccan dialect, often in the same message, and no source checked says anything about that mix for Muse.

The listing covers one of the two. The Muse US App Store listing shows English plus 38 other languages, and Arabic is on that list, according to a September 2026 guide. French is not named in the sources checked for this guide, so this guide will not claim French is on the Muse listing. A listing is not confirmed support in any case: it says the store page covers the language, and it says nothing about quality in real conversations.

The French angle has a useful comparison. If Muse launches in France with strong French, Morocco benefits from the same language work; if French lags there, it will lag in Morocco too. That is reasoning, not a promise, and it is labeled as one.

What are Morocco's data protection rules?

Morocco has one of Africa's oldest data protection laws. The data protection law enacted on February 18, 2009, is Morocco's principal data protection legislation, modeled on the EU Data Protection Directive of 1995, and Morocco was among the first countries in Africa and the Arab world to adopt a comprehensive framework, according to the CMS expert guide. The regulator is the CNDP (Commission Nationale de contrôle de la protection des Données à caractère Personnel).

The law has teeth that matter for an agent. Before processing personal data, most controllers must file a declaration with the CNDP, and processing sensitive data requires prior authorization, according to a law firm summary updated October 2026. Transfers abroad are allowed only to a country with sufficient protection or under listed exceptions such as express consent or CNDP authorization, according to an AI rules review last checked October 26, 2026. Fines run from 10,000 to 100,000 dirhams for processing without the required declaration or authorization.

There is no AI law in force in Morocco as of October 26, 2026, according to the same review, which notes a government framework law on AI reported as a draft and a Maroc IA 2030 roadmap presented on September 8, 2026. Law 09-08 is therefore the rule that applies today. This guide does not claim the law is why Muse is absent: Meta has cited no Moroccan legal reason. The law is context for your settings choices when Muse arrives, not a launch explanation.

Does being close to Europe help Morocco?

No. Rollouts follow a country list, not a map. Morocco sits a short strait from Spain, trades heavily with Europe, and shares French as a daily language with France, and none of that moves Morocco up Meta's list. The five launch countries are the US, Canada, Japan, Singapore, and Mexico, a list that follows Meta's own order, not geography, and Morocco is not on it.

The EU rules do not govern Morocco either. The GDPR, the AI Act, and the Digital Markets Act are the framework the EU brings into play for a Muse launch, according to a guide to using Muse outside the US, which notes Meta AI itself took over a year to reach Europe with reduced features. Those rules apply inside the EU and EEA. Morocco has its own law, its own regulator, the CNDP, and its own timeline, and a Muse launch in Morocco would be governed by Moroccan rules, not European ones.

Proximity helps in one narrow way: language work. French language quality built for France, Belgium, or Switzerland transfers to Morocco's French speaking users, and Arabic work built for the Gulf or Egypt transfers to Morocco's Arabic speakers. But language work is not a launch, and no source checked describes any Morocco specific language or connector work by Meta. Closeness to Europe is a reason for optimism about quality, not about timing.

Can a VPN unlock Muse in Morocco?

Every door has the same gate, so no surface offers a shortcut. The iPhone app, the Android app, the website, and the Muse chat inside WhatsApp all follow the same country list, according to a guide to using Muse outside the US. WhatsApp does not help, the web does not help, and a different app does not help, because the check runs against your account and region on all of them.

A September 2026 check of 15 storefronts besides the US found the Muse iPhone listing in none of them, and the author concluded the gate sits at the account layer, not the web layer. That is why a VPN disappoints: it moves your apparent network location and nothing else, while your App Store country, your account region, and the region Meta has on file stay Moroccan. Mixed reports from people who tried are exactly what an account layer gate predicts.

Count the cost before testing a workaround. Your Meta account may hold your WhatsApp chats, your Instagram, and your Facebook identity, and workarounds of this kind are unverified and may break Meta terms. No source confirms Meta bans accounts for VPN use, and no source confirms it is safe, so the warning stays as caution, never as verified fact. An app arriving on its own schedule is not worth staking that account on.

Which privacy setting should you change first?

Morocco's consent focused law makes the first settings check a personal one. In Muse, open Settings, then Data controls, and switch off Help improve our AI models, tapping "Turn off" to confirm, on the day you create your account and before your first long chat. The switch decides whether your Muse interactions help improve Meta's AI models, and verifying it yourself beats assuming it.

Law 09-08 shares many principles with the GDPR but places greater emphasis on consent and regulatory filings, according to a 2026 guide to the law. The in app toggle is the version of consent you control yourself, and it takes less than a minute to verify. The law governs what Meta must declare and obtain; the toggle governs what you allow.

After the switch, slow down on connections. A connector is not a setting to enable and forget: each one opens another window into your mail, calendar, and messages, so add them one at a time, starting with the apps your first tasks need. Law 09-08 and the CNDP sit behind you as the regulatory backstop, and the direct control is Help improve our AI models, switched off before your first long chat.
