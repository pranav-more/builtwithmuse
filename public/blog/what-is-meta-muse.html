What is Muse, and how do invite codes work?

A plain introduction to Meta's Muse, why access currently runs on invites, and how the community pool helps you get in and give back.

Muse is Meta's AI app. Right now it is not something you can simply download and open: access is handed out through invites, and each person who gets in receives a small number of codes to pass on. That is why so many people are searching for a working code, and why this site exists.

How the invite system works

The mechanics are simple once you have seen them from both sides.

  • Someone inside Muse has an invite code and an invite link tied to their account.
  • You redeem that code inside the app, under Settings, and your account is unlocked.
  • The person who invited you is rewarded inside Muse when their code is used. The size of that reward changes, so treat any number you see online as a rumour rather than a promise.
  • Once you are in, you receive codes of your own and the cycle continues.

The catch is that a code only works a limited number of times. Post one publicly and it is gone within minutes, usually before most people even see it.

Where the community pool comes in

Built with Muse is a shared pool. People who already have access add their codes; people who want access take one. To keep it fair we hand each code to at most two people, then move to the next one, and the newest submissions go out first so nobody's code gets buried. If a code turns out not to work, a couple of reports retire it automatically.

There is no sign up and no queue. Press the button, copy the code, redeem it, and please come back and add your own invite once you are inside. The pool only stays useful if it is refilled.

A few things worth knowing Codes go fast, so redeem yours soon after you take it. Never pay anyone for a code. And be careful with sites that ask for your Meta password in exchange for access: Muse invites never need it.

Not in your country yet?

Muse is rolling out region by region. If it is not available where you live, join the waitlist on the home page with your country and we will let you know when codes for your region start appearing in the pool.
