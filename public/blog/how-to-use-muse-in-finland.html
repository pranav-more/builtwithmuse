Is Muse available in Finland?

As of November 13, 2026, the answer is no. Muse is live in five countries: the US, since September 8, 2026; Canada, since September 18; Japan, since October 23; Singapore, since October 28; and Mexico, since November 5. Finland is not among them, and neither is any other EU or EEA country. See which countries have Muse for the full list and the launch dates.

Europe's absence is stated bluntly in coverage. A November 7, 2026 report puts it in one line: Europe is not on the list, and Meta has not given a date. A second report the same week notes that Spaces is available wherever Muse is available, and that no EU country was on the list as of November 7, 2026. Finland waits with the whole continent, not alone.

The experience of trying is the same block message seen everywhere Muse has not launched: "Muse isn't available in your country or region yet. We'll let you know when it's available to you." That wording comes from a September 2026 VPN guide that quotes the message in full. There is no waitlist to join and no signup queue, so the message is the whole status update Finland gets.

Why does Finland wait if it leads Europe in AI adoption?

Finland is the EU's most enthusiastic adopter of AI, and it still has no Muse. Two thirds of Finnish firms use generative AI tools such as ChatGPT, Bard, or Copilot, the highest share in the EU and far above the EU average of 37 percent, according to the European Investment Bank's 2025 Investment Survey. Eurostat's 2025 data tells the same story from a different angle: 37.82 percent of Finnish enterprises used AI, second only to Denmark at 42.03 percent, against an EU average of 19.95 percent, in Eurostat enterprise figures.

Adoption does not set Meta's launch order. Meta has said nothing about demand in Finland, Finnish users, or a Finland date, and this guide will not turn a survey ranking into a launch hint. What Meta has said, through Zuckerberg on the October 21, 2026 earnings call, is that Muse is compute intensive to run and that the rollout pace reflects both running it efficiently and tailoring it to each country, including work on connectors, according to coverage of the call. A ready market and a ready product are different things, and only Meta knows which side is gating Finland.

The practical takeaway is patience with preparation. Finland's high adoption means Finns will likely adopt Muse fast when it arrives, and it means the Finnish language question matters more here than in markets where English dominates daily work. Both points argue for having an account plan ready, not for expecting an early launch.

Will Muse speak Finnish?

Unconfirmed, like every language question about Muse. Meta has published no language list for Muse, and no source checked confirms how well Muse handles Finnish in practice. Anyone who tells you Muse speaks fluent Finnish today is guessing, because the product is not available anywhere Finnish is the daily language.

The encouraging signs come from Meta AI, not Muse. Finnish appears among the additional languages for Meta AI on AI glasses, provided through Meta's partners, on Meta's own help page, and live translation on Meta glasses supports 20 languages including Finnish, according to a June 2026 report. Those are glasses features for the simpler assistant, and they prove Finnish is on Meta's language roadmap, not that Muse speaks it.

The caution comes from the EU launch of Meta AI itself. When it reached Europe in March 2025, it launched in six languages only: English, French, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese, and German, and Finnish was not among them, according to launch coverage. Finnish support arrived later, through glasses and expanded language lists. A Muse launch in Finland could easily repeat that order: English first, Finnish quality unknown, Finnish fluency later. Plan for English on day one.

What Meta AI and glasses already do in Finland

Finland got Meta AI on glasses early by European standards. On April 23, 2025, Meta expanded Meta AI on its glasses to seven more EU countries including Finland, so people there could ask Meta AI questions by voice, according to Meta's own announcement. Finland also appears on the June 2026 Meta Glasses sales list, so the hardware is sold there, unlike in several other unlaunched Muse markets.

Meta AI inside WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, and Messenger reached Finland with the March 2025 EU rollout, which covered all 27 EU member states plus 14 more European countries, according to a review of availability. That assistant answers questions and helps with text. It does not book, buy, call, or act on your behalf, which is the whole point of Muse. The difference between Muse and Meta AI is the difference between an answer and an action.

None of this shortens the Muse wait, but it does mean Finns are not starting from zero. If you already use Meta AI by voice on glasses, or in WhatsApp, you know Meta's assistant style and its limits. Muse adds agency: connectors, actions, and memory across tasks. When it launches in Finland, the learning curve will be about what to delegate, not about how to talk to an AI.

Why does Europe wait longer than the US?

The short answer is that Europe made Meta wait before, and Meta remembers. In 2024, Meta kept its multimodal model out of the EU, citing an unpredictable regulatory environment, and Meta AI only reached Europe in March 2025, according to a November 2026 report citing Euronews. The delay was regulatory from the start: Ireland's Data Protection Commission asked Meta to pause training plans, and the rollout only moved after a revised plan was approved.

The timeline since then shows a regulator and a company negotiating in public. Meta planned a broader European rollout in 2024 but hit a wall when the Irish regulator asked it to pause training on public EU posts; in May 2025, the regulator approved a revised training plan with an easier objection process, and Meta began training on public EU posts, according to the same availability review. Finland, as an EU member, inherits every step of that negotiation, including the caution it breeds.

The EU AI Act adds a second layer. Core obligations for general purpose AI models applied from August 2, 2025, and Article 50 transparency duties apply from August 2, 2026, with a grace period for machine readable marking to December 2, 2026, according to an October 2026 analysis. No EU country has Muse, so Finland's position is the EU position. For how the same wait looks in the EU's largest market, see how to use Muse in Germany.

What would Muse cost in Finland?

Finland has no price for Muse in any currency, because Meta has announced no euro pricing at all. What circulates is the reported US shape: a free tier with a weekly usage limit, then Power and Maximum, which buy more usage rather than more features, at $20 and $100 a month. The full pricing breakdown explains each tier and where the free tier ends.

One number in that picture needs a source note. The 100 million tokens a week figure is from Mark Zuckerberg's launch statement, and it does not appear in Meta's help pages, so it is a launch claim, not a documented guarantee. The shape is the documented part: free with a weekly limit, with subscriptions adding usage. On that shape, a Finland launch would give everyday users the free tier and reserve the paid tiers for people who run Muse all day.

Do not guess a euro price from the dollar price. Taxes, App Store tiers, and local pricing decisions all move the number, and Meta has announced nothing for Finland or the EU. A euro price, when it exists, will show on Meta pricing pages and in the Finnish App Store listing. Until then, any euro figure attached to Muse in Finland is invented.

Can a VPN unlock Muse in Finland?

No verified method exists, and this guide will not invent one. The block message itself hints at why: the gate checks where your account belongs, not just where your traffic appears to come from. A September 2026 technical check found the Muse iPhone listing returning results only in the US App Store across 15 other storefronts, and concluded the gate sits at the account layer, not the web layer. A VPN changes your apparent location. It does not change your App Store country or your account region.

Reports from people who tried are mixed and second hand. Some describe getting through with a US account plus a VPN; others describe the same block message with a VPN running. Neither report is verified, Meta publishes no statement blessing the workaround, and a success screen from one account on one day is not a method. Treat every tutorial that promises access as a claim to test at your own risk, not as instructions.

Think about what one Meta account holds before testing a workaround. WhatsApp, Instagram, and Facebook can all sit behind the same login, and one guide calls VPN workarounds a gamble with that account. No source confirms Meta bans accounts for VPN use, and no source confirms it is safe, so this guide keeps the warning as caution, not verified fact. A tutorial shows the upside; it rarely prices the downside.

Which privacy setting should you change first?

The first check costs less than a minute and should happen before your first long chat. In Muse, go to Settings, then Data controls, find Help improve our AI models, and switch it off, tapping "Turn off" to confirm. That switch decides whether your Muse interactions help improve Meta's AI models, and the EU history in the section above is the reason to verify it yourself instead of assuming it is off.

That history is the Irish regulator story from the section above: a pause on training plans in 2024, a revised plan approved in May 2025 with an easier objection process, and training on public EU posts after that. The objection process exists because regulators required it, and the in app toggle is the version you control yourself. Help improve our AI models is the first setting to verify, and it takes less than a minute.

After the toggle, be deliberate about connectors. Each app you connect gives Muse more to see and more to act on, which is the point, and also the risk. Connect the two or three apps your first tasks need, use Muse for a week, and add more only when a task asks for them. The data sharing setting comes before the connectors, not after, because it governs everything Muse learns from every connected app.

What can you do in Finland today?

Use Meta AI where it already lives: in WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, and Messenger, and by voice on glasses if you own a pair. The EU version launched with a reduced feature set, and gaps remain by app and country, so check what your apps offer rather than assuming the US feature list. The habit that transfers to Muse is describing a task in plain language and checking the result, and Meta AI is a fine place to build it.

Two features define Muse today, and both are worth knowing before launch day. Voice is the talking avatar that reached all US users on November 5, 2026, and began as US only, so Finland may open without it. Spaces are the custom mini apps that live wherever Muse lives, which today means the five launch countries and nowhere else. Day one goes to building if you already know both, and to reading if you do not.

The account check deserves its own preparation. Age verification problems include stricter checks and card declines, and the rule behind them is Muse's own: 18 and older. The App Store listing shows a 17+ rating, which is Apple's highest tier and a different rule from Muse's. Keep a payment card ready and allow more time for the check than for the install.
