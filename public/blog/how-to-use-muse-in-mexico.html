How to Use Meta Muse in Mexico

Muse launched in Mexico on November 5, 2026. The fifth country to get it, the first in Latin America, and the first where it speaks Spanish. Get the official app on iPhone or Android, open muse.ai on the web, or find it in WhatsApp. No VPN, no store tricks, no workarounds. Yes, Muse is live in Mexico. Meta launched it on November 5, 2026, the fifth country after the US, Canada, Japan and Singapore, and the first in Latin America. It works in Spanish, for users 18 and over, on iPhone, Android, muse.ai and WhatsApp.

When did Muse launch in Mexico?

November 5, 2026. Alexandr Wang, Meta chief AI officer, announced it on X the same day, with the official Muse account, and an announcement video showing the Muse character on a trajinera boat under a México arch. The launch order is the US on September 8, Canada on September 18, Japan on October 23, Singapore on October 27, and Mexico on November 5, 2026. Mexico is the first country in Latin America to get Muse, and the first Spanish speaking market.

Mexico had already appeared in French and German language posts from Meta Connect in September, listed alongside the US and Canada. That was not a launch. Trackers still called Mexico unconfirmed as of October 31, and November 5 is the first dedicated launch announcement. If you read a guide dated before November 5 that says Muse is available in Mexico, it was written from that mention, not from a launch.

The announcement came with no dedicated Meta Newsroom post. The launch was announced on X and covered the same day by Mexican press, including Infobae and Geekzilla, and by All Things Muse.

1. Get Muse from the official place

You do not need a country trick to start in Mexico. Use the official app or website, create your account, then redeem a code if you want free Muse tokens.

Choose the route that fits the device you already use.

On iPhone

Open the official Muse app in the App Store. Install it, open it, and follow the sign up steps on screen.

On Android

Open the Google Play Store on your phone. Find the official Muse app from Meta. Install it, open it, and follow the sign up steps on screen.

On the web

Open muse.ai in your browser. Follow the sign up steps there. This is useful if you want to try Muse before you install an app, or if you are on a computer. The Mac app was not named in the Mexico launch coverage, so the web is the route to use on a Mac for now.

On WhatsApp

Muse is also available inside WhatsApp in Mexico. Open WhatsApp and find Muse there to start a chat without installing anything new. It is the same agent, in the app you already use every day.

2. Do you need an invite code in Mexico?

No. No invite code or waitlist is mentioned in any launch coverage for Mexico. Every outlet describes signup the same way: open muse.ai or download the app and set up the agent. No code is needed to sign up in the US or Canada either. The code gate belonged to the closed beta before the US launch on September 8, and it no longer exists.

The code is still worth having, as a reward, not a gate. When a new person joins with a code and redeems it in Settings within 48 hours, both people get 1 billion Muse tokens.

  1. Open Muse and finish the sign up steps.
  2. Open Settings after your account is ready.
  3. Find the invite code area.
  4. Enter your code and complete the redemption.
  5. Do this within 48 hours of joining so both people can get the reward.

If one code does not work, return to the community pool and try a different available code. Do not wait until the 48 hour window is almost over.

3. What can you connect in Mexico?

Seven Mexican services connect to Muse at launch, plus the Google and Instagram connectors that work everywhere. The local seven cover the everyday jobs: entertainment, shopping, travel, cars, money, and food delivery.

  • Cinépolis. Check movie showtimes and cinema experiences.
  • Coppel. Shop and use Coppel retail services inside Muse.
  • Despegar. Manage travel options, flights and stays.
  • Kavak. Tools for buying and selling cars.
  • Kueski. Financial services, credit and loans.
  • Liverpool. Discover products and shop.
  • Rappi. Food and everyday delivery.

Gmail, Google Calendar and Instagram connect too. Gmail for email, Calendar for your schedule, and Instagram for saved content you want Muse to use.

One caution: connectors switch on gradually. Some are not live on day one for every account. Check Muse settings to see which ones work on yours, and check again after a few days if one you want is missing.

4. What can you do with Muse in Mexico?

Start with a job that uses a connector, because that is where Muse stops being a chatbot and starts being useful. Ask it to find a movie time at Cinépolis for Saturday night, then build the evening around it. Ask Despegar to compare flights and stays for a weekend trip, with the dates and the budget you actually have. Ask Liverpool to find a product and compare it before you buy. Ask Rappi to handle tonight dinner order while you finish something else.

The money and car connectors reward a clear question even more. Ask Kueski what a loan looks like for the amount and term you have in mind, and read the answer before you commit to anything. Ask Kavak what your car is worth, or what a car you are eyeing should cost, and use the answer to check the seller, not to replace your own judgment.

Outside the connectors, the everyday jobs are the same as anywhere else. Connect Gmail and ask Muse to clear the inbox down to the five messages that need you today. Connect Calendar and ask it to find a free hour this week for the thing you keep putting off. Use Instagram to pull up saved posts you want to act on, instead of scrolling past them again.

One rule covers all of it: give Muse the facts it needs in the first message. The date, the budget, the city, the constraint. A vague ask gets a vague answer, and a precise ask gets work you can use.

5. Does Muse speak Spanish?

Yes. Mexico is the first Spanish speaking market for Muse, and Spanish works from day one. Talk to it in Spanish and it answers in Spanish. You can mix Spanish and English in the same chat, and it follows you. If an answer comes back in English when you wanted Spanish, say so in Spanish and it switches.

This matters more than it looks. Most AI products arrive in Mexico in English first and add Spanish later, if ever. Muse arrives with Spanish as a launch language, which is part of why the Mexico launch matters beyond Mexico: it is the first proof the product can live in Spanish, and the next Spanish speaking launches will follow this one.

6. Use your first hour well

You will learn more by finishing one useful task than by testing random prompts. Start small and use something from your real life.

  • Check your privacy setting. Open Settings and turn off Help improve our AI models if you do not want your data shared to help improve the models. This is a privacy caution. It does not hide your location or change country access.
  • Give Muse one clear task. Say what you want, what matters, and what it must ask before doing.
  • Check the result. Fix anything that is wrong before you use or share it.
  • Save the method that worked. You can use it again instead of starting from zero.

Muse runs in its own secure, isolated cloud computer with its own browser. Sending an email or completing a purchase needs your explicit permission, so nothing leaves your account without you saying so.

Need ideas for the first hour? Clear five emails with Gmail, find a movie time with Cinépolis, compare one product on Liverpool, and ask Muse to plan tomorrow from your calendar. Four small wins teach you more about the agent than an afternoon of test prompts.

Need an idea for your first useful task? Browse Muse workflows made by the community. Pick one that matches a real job you need to finish.

7. What does Muse cost in Mexico?

Basic use is free, with optional paid subscription plans. Meta has not published Mexico prices, so this guide states no price for Mexico. Do not trust a site that quotes US plan prices as Mexico prices: no source confirms they apply here.

Start free. The free tier covers everyday use, and the token reward from an invite code adds to it: redeem a code within 48 hours of joining and you get 1 billion Muse tokens. You can look at the paid plans inside the app when you know what you actually use Muse for. Our Muse tokens guide explains what tokens buy and how fast everyday use spends them.

8. What is still coming?

Three things are not here yet. Meta AI glasses compatibility is coming soon, announced as próximamente in the launch coverage. Mexico was the first country in Latin America where the glasses went on sale, in September, so the full agent arrives where the hardware is already established. Muse Voice, the live voice conversations with an expressive avatar, is US only for now, with no Mexico date given. And there are no dates for the UK, the EU, or the rest of Latin America. Meta help pages say only that the service is not yet available everywhere.

9. If the app does not appear yet

The rollout may not reach every Mexican App Store listing at the same moment. If the official app is missing, wait for the rollout and check again later. Do not change your store country, use a VPN, or install a file from an unknown website.

You can also try muse.ai while you wait. If the website does not open for your account, wait and try again later. A missing app or blocked account does not prove that Mexico is unsupported.
