This week

Get what you need from Instagram without opening it, by Mike. Muse went and found three reels with yapping frameworks, and I never opened Instagram once. I got the advice I needed, shot my reel, and saved the reels for later.

Everyone knows the trap this one solves. You open Instagram for one specific thing and surface thirty minutes later without it, deep in a feed that was designed to keep you there. Mike needed reels carrying frameworks for his own videos, so instead of going in himself he sent Muse in. It found the three reels, saved them for later, and reported back. It is the agent pattern at its simplest: a job with a clear finish line, handled by the agent while the human stays out of the feed entirely.

How featuring works

Publish a workflow on Built with Muse and it is in the pool for the next pick. The editors read every new workflow. Once a week one is chosen and featured: it leads the homepage for the week, carries a Featured badge on its page, and is added to this page for good.

What gets picked: a specific job, a prompt a reader can copy without guesswork, and an honest result, including the parts that did not work. What never gets picked: anything vague, anything borrowed, and anything that reads like an advertisement.

Featured creators are told first, before their week starts, and get a link they can share. If you would rather not be featured, say so on your workflow and it will be skipped, no questions asked.
