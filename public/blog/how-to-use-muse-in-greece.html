How to Use Muse in Greece

Muse is not available in Greece yet, and Meta has announced no launch date. Muse is live in five countries: the US, Canada, Japan, Singapore, and Mexico. Greece is not on that list, and Meta publishes no country list and no timeline for what comes next.

Is Muse available in Greece?

As of November 13, 2026, Muse is live in five countries: the US, where it launched on September 8, 2026; Canada, on September 18; Japan, on October 23; Singapore, on October 28; and Mexico, on November 5, the fifth market and the first in Latin America. Greece is not one of them. A November 2026 availability roundup lists the same five countries and notes that Meta has announced no launch dates for the UK, Europe, India, or Pakistan.

Greece is covered by the same EEA wide absence. An October 8, 2026 status check records Muse as not available in the EU, the UK, Switzerland, or the rest of the EEA, and a separate check of 22 App Stores found Muse not listed in the EU storefronts it covered, including Germany, France, Spain, Italy, the Netherlands, and Sweden. Meta help wording quoted in that check describes Muse as in limited testing and not available in all locations yet.

If you open Muse from Greece today, expect the message seen in screenshots from unlaunched markets: "Muse isn't available in your country or region yet." The full wording adds that Meta will let you know when it is available to you. There is no official waitlist, no signup queue, and no country list you can check, so the message is the only status Greece gets.

Why is there no launch date for Greece?

Meta has given no reason specific to Greece, and none should be invented. What exists is a Europe wide pattern. Meta AI, the simpler assistant, took nearly 18 months to reach Europe after its US debut, launching across the EU on March 20, 2025, and Meta kept its multimodal model out of the EU in 2024, citing an unpredictable regulatory environment.

The rules behind that caution are the GDPR and the EU AI Act. The AI Act core obligations for general purpose AI models applied from August 2, 2025, and Article 50 transparency duties apply from August 2, 2026, with a grace period for machine readable marking running to December 2, 2026. An agent that reads your mail and spends your money raises questions under the GDPR and the AI Act that a chatbot does not.

Regulation is only part of the story. On the October 21, 2026 earnings call, Mark Zuckerberg described Muse as compute intensive to run, and said the delay was partly about running it efficiently and partly about tailoring it to each country, including work on connectors, according to coverage of the call. Meta has not said which factor gates Greece, so any guide that names one cause for Greece is guessing.

Will Muse speak Greek?

Nobody can confirm this yet, and this guide will not pretend otherwise. Meta has published no language list for Muse, a point one October 2026 guide makes explicitly after checking Meta announcements. Until Meta publishes a list or Muse launches in Greece, Greek support is an open question, not a promise.

The groundwork exists on paper. The Muse US App Store listing shows English plus 38 other languages, and Greek is on that list, alongside Arabic, Hebrew, and Turkish. Treat that as a listing, not as confirmed support: an App Store language list says the store page is translated, and it says nothing about how well the agent itself handles Greek in practice.

The Meta AI precedent cuts both ways. When Meta AI reached Europe in March 2025, it launched in six languages only: English, French, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese, and German, according to contemporary coverage. Greek was not among them. Greek does now appear on WhatsApp's list of languages for Meta AI access, which shows Greek support arrived for the simpler assistant. Whether Muse matches that on day one in Greece is unconfirmed.

What about Muse inside WhatsApp in Greece?

No. The country list applies across every way you can reach Muse: the iPhone app, the Android app, the Muse website, and the Muse chat inside WhatsApp, as one guide to using Muse outside the US puts it. Opening WhatsApp does not bypass the gate, because the gate checks your account and region, not which app you open.

That matters in Greece because WhatsApp is a daily habit there. Sensor Tower put WhatsApp weekly active users in Greece at about 2.7 million at the end of December 2025, after a dip from 3.2 million earlier in the quarter, in its Q4 2025 Greece report. The same top lists include Viber and Telegram, so Greek messaging is split across several apps, but WhatsApp is where most people would first look for a Muse chat.

What does work inside WhatsApp in Greece is Meta AI, the chat assistant, available since the March 2025 EU rollout. It is not Muse. Muse and Meta AI are different products: one chats, the other acts, and only the acting one is missing in Greece. If you want the WhatsApp details, read how Muse works inside WhatsApp so you know what to expect when the gate lifts.

Can a VPN unlock Muse in Greece?

Treat every tutorial that promises this as unverified. The reason is structural: the gate sits at the account layer, not the web layer, according to a September 2026 technical check that found the Muse iPhone listing returning results only in the US App Store across 15 other storefronts. A VPN changes the network address your traffic appears to come from. It does not change your App Store country, your account region, or the region Meta has on file for you.

That is why VPN results are mixed in reports, and why no guide here promises a method works. Some people report getting through with a US account and a VPN; others report the same block message with a VPN running. Both reports are second hand, neither is verified, and Meta publishes no statement blessing the workaround. If a tutorial shows a success screen, remember it shows one account on one day, not a method that holds.

The risk to weigh is your Meta account itself. VPN and US account workarounds are unverified and may break Meta terms, one guide warns, calling them a gamble with your Meta account. This guide words that as caution, not verified fact: no source confirms Meta bans accounts for VPN use, and no source confirms it is safe. If the account holds your WhatsApp, Instagram, and Facebook life, do not stake it on an unverified workaround for an app that will arrive on its own schedule.

What would Muse cost in Greece?

There is no Greece price, no euro price, and no announcement of either. The only pricing in circulation is the reported US pricing: free with a weekly usage limit, Power at $20 a month, and Maximum at $100 a month, with paid plans buying usage capacity rather than extra features. See whether Muse is free for the full breakdown of what each tier buys.

The free tier deserves a precise note. The figure of 100 million tokens a week comes from Mark Zuckerberg's launch statement, not from Meta help pages, so attribute it that way and do not treat it as a documented guarantee. What Meta help pages do confirm is the shape: a free tier with a weekly limit, and subscriptions that add usage. If a Greece launch follows the US shape, the free tier is where most Greeks would start, and the paid tiers would matter only for heavy use.

Currency is the open question. Meta has announced no euro pricing for Muse, and no source checked gives a Greece figure. Do not convert $20 at the day rate and call it the Greece price: app pricing, taxes, and local tiers all move the number, and Meta has said nothing. When a euro price exists, it will appear on Meta pricing pages and in the Greek App Store listing, and this guide will be updated to match.

Which privacy setting should you change first?

Open Settings, then Data controls, and turn off Help improve our AI models, confirming with "Turn off". Do this on the day you create your account, before your first long chat. The setting controls whether your interactions with Muse help improve Meta's AI models, and checking it early means your early chats are not feeding a system you never agreed to feed.

Greece sits under the GDPR, and the EU history here is instructive. Ireland's Data Protection Commission, Meta's lead privacy regulator in the EU, asked Meta to pause plans to train AI models on public Facebook and Instagram posts from European users, and in May 2025 it approved a revised training plan with an easier objection process, after which Meta began training on public EU posts, according to a review of the rollout. The lesson for Greece is that your objection rights exist, but they are exercised through settings and objection forms, not assumed.

The toggle is not the whole privacy story. Help improve our AI models is the setting to check first, but also read what Muse can access through each connector you connect, and connect only what the task needs. An agent with your mail, calendar, and messages connected is useful precisely because it sees a lot, and that is exactly why the data sharing setting comes before the connectors, not after.

What can you do in Greece today?

Start with Meta AI, which is already in your WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, and Messenger apps in Greece after the March 2025 EU rollout. It launched with a reduced feature set in Europe: the EU version could not generate images or creatively distort selfies at launch, functions already available in the US, according to launch coverage. Check what your apps offer today, because the feature set has expanded since, and the gaps that remain vary by app and country.

Do not buy hardware expecting Muse. Live translation on Meta glasses supports 20 languages including Greek, according to a June 2026 report, but Greece was not on the sales list in that same report, which covered the US, the UK, Canada, Australia, and several European countries. Glasses in Greece would be an import with no local Muse story attached, so wait for a Greece specific announcement before spending.

Use the wait to learn the product. Read the list of Muse connectors so you know which apps Muse can reach, and think about which two or three you would connect first. When Muse launches in Greece, the people who get value fastest will be the ones who already know what an agent can and cannot do, not the ones discovering it on day one.

What would launch day look like in Greece?

Launch day in Greece would show up in three places: the Greek App Store listing for Muse from Meta, the disappearance of the country block message, and a Meta announcement. The iPhone listing carries id 6760173601, according to the September 2026 check, and its absence from the Greek storefront is the most direct signal that Greece is still waiting. Check the listing, not a rumor account: if the listing is there and installs, Greece is live.

Do not pay anyone for early access. There is no official waitlist and no country signup queue, so anyone selling a Greece launch list, a guaranteed invite, or early access is not Meta. The same caution applies to codes: buying a Muse invite code is a market this site warns against, because codes are meant to be shared free between members. A launch brings free entry through the open entry point, and a code is a bonus, not a ticket.

Age checks are part of launch day too. Muse's own rule is 18 and older, while the App Store listing carries a 17+ rating, which is Apple's highest age tier, not Muse's rule. Keep a payment card ready if age verification asks for one, and expect the check to be stricter than the app install itself.

Will Muse come to glasses and watches in Greece?

Muse on the wrist was announced on November 9, 2026, by Alexandr Wang, with the iOS app at version 15.0 covering Apple Watch and Wear OS. No country list accompanied the announcement, and no source checked names Greece for watches. Treat wearables as a second rollout that follows the phone launch, not something that arrives first.

Glasses are further out for Greece. Zuckerberg said on the October 21, 2026 earnings call that Muse comes to Meta glasses starting next month, with no countries named, according to coverage of the call. Greece was not on the June 2026 Meta Glasses sales list, so a glasses launch in Greece needs both a hardware sale and a Muse rollout, and neither is announced.

Voice is the feature most likely to confuse launch day expectations. Muse Voice rolled out to all users in the US on November 5, 2026, and is US only to start, according to a November 7, 2026 report. A Greece launch may therefore start without Voice, and the talking avatar Greeks see in US videos may lag the Greek launch by weeks or months. That is a guess about order, not a fact about dates, and it is labeled as one.
