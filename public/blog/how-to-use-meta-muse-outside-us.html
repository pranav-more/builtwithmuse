2. Path one: web on a laptop

This is the most tested route and the fastest one. The short version: open Chrome or Brave on a laptop, install any VPN and connect it to the United States, go to muse.ai, leave the age verification screen alone, and redeem your invite code in Settings.

Most people get in on the first try. If you hit the region block instead, the full guide walks through every fix: fresh IP addresses, fresh browser profiles, the fresh start rule, and the last resort of switching devices. Read the full laptop guide.

3. Path two: the iPhone app

The Muse app lives on the US App Store, so your local App Store simply does not list it. The clean fix is a second Apple ID set to the United States, used only for the App Store, with the payment method set to None. No US credit card, no changes to your main account. Then a VPN connected to the US before you open the app, because the service checks your location at runtime too.

Prefer to wait for the official launch? Join the waitlist and we will send you a fresh code on launch day.

Every step, including what to do when the App Store still shows your local store, is in the detailed walkthrough. Read the full iPhone guide.

4. What does not work, so you do not waste time

  • Doing it all on your phone browser. Phones get flagged or blocked far more often than laptops. Use a computer for the web method.
  • Sharing your phone VPN over a hotspot. The VPN usually does not carry over to hotspot connections, so the laptop still shows your real location.
  • Trying to pass age verification. It will not work from outside the US. Skip the screen and move on.
  • Changing the IP in your wifi settings. That number is only for your home network. You need a fresh public IP, which a new VPN server gives you.

Each of these is explained with its fix in the laptop troubleshooting guide.
