How to Use Muse in Brazil

Muse has not launched in Brazil, and Meta has not announced a date. Get the status in seconds, pick your path below for laptop, iPhone, or Mac, see what does not work so you do not waste time, and the privacy setting to turn off once you are in.

1. The status: not available in Brazil

As of September 2026, Muse is available in the United States and Canada only. Brazil is not on the list, and Meta has not announced a launch date for Brazil.

That is the whole status. Everything below is what you can do about it today.

One thing in your favor: Muse also works inside WhatsApp, with a smaller feature set than the full app. Since WhatsApp is already on your phone, that path skips the App Store steps entirely. You still need a Muse account first, which is what the guides below walk through.

A quick note on VPNs: rules differ by country, so check what applies where you live. This guide covers the technical steps only.

3. What does not work, so you do not waste time

  • Doing everything in a phone browser. The signup does not complete on a mobile browser. Use a computer for the laptop method.
  • Sharing a phone VPN over a hotspot. The VPN on your phone does not extend to the devices on its hotspot. Install the VPN on the computer itself.
  • Trying to pass age verification. It does not work as a way in from outside the US. Do not waste an evening on it.
  • Changing the IP in your wifi settings. That number is only for your home network. It does not change your visible location. The VPN handles that.

4. Once you are in: turn this setting off

Do this before anything else. Open the Muse app, go to Settings, then Data controls, and turn off Help improve our AI models. That switch controls whether Meta may use your chats to train future models.

One caution: you are using Muse from a country where it has not launched. If Meta ever reviews your account and sees the mismatch, the account can get flagged or banned. Turning this off does not hide your location, but it keeps your chats out of their training data.

5. Rather wait for the official launch?

Fair choice. Join the waitlist and you will get a fresh invite code the day Muse launches in Brazil. No setup, no workarounds, and the code arrives exactly when you can use it.
