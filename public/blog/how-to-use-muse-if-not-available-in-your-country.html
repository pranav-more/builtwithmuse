How to use Muse if it is not available in your country (2026 guide)

September 2026 · 5 minute read

Muse has not launched everywhere yet, and the number one question we hear is simple: "It has not launched in my country, so what do I do now?"

A creator who regularly posts Muse walkthroughs answered the four questions his viewers ask the most: age verification getting stuck, Muse showing as unavailable in their country, whether a VPN actually works, and which browser to use. This guide puts his answers into plain, step by step words.

1. Age verification keeps popping up

You can skip it.

If the age verification screen appears and you do not want to complete it, just leave it and continue. It does not block you from using Muse. Plenty of users get past this screen by simply moving on; it will not lock you out of your account.

2. Muse says "not available in my country"

Use a VPN. That is the full answer.

Connect to a country where Muse is live (the United States or Canada work) and then open Muse in your browser. In the creator's video he uses a free VPN extension inside Chrome, so you do not need a paid VPN app to try this.

Here is the exact setup:

  1. Open Chrome on your computer.
  2. Install a free VPN extension from the Chrome Web Store.
  3. Set the VPN location to the United States or Canada.
  4. Turn the VPN on, then go to muse.ai.
  5. Sign up or log in as normal.

Once you are connected through a supported country, Muse loads and works the way it does for everyone else.

3. Which browser should I use?

Chrome.

The creator does the entire walkthrough in Chrome with the VPN extension installed, and everything works there. Any modern browser should technically be fine, but Chrome is the tested, proven path. If you run into trouble in another browser, switch to Chrome first before troubleshooting anything else.

4. What do you get once you are in?

From the settings walkthrough in the video, here is how the Muse plan works:

  • The free plan has a weekly usage limit. It resets every week, so you get a fresh allowance regularly. Pace your heavier sessions and you will rarely hit the wall.
  • Additional tokens never expire. If you claim extra tokens through an invite code, they stay in your account until you use them. There is no countdown.
  • Redeem your invite code under Settings in the Muse app, or on the web at muse.ai.
The short version Skip the age check, turn on a free VPN set to a supported country, open muse.ai in Chrome, and redeem an invite code. That is the whole setup the creator demonstrates.
