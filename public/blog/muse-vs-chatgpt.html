Muse vs ChatGPT: Which One Should You Use?

Muse and ChatGPT overlap more than the hype suggests. For most everyday tasks they do the same job, so the real decision comes down to availability, access, and which apps you already live in. Here is the honest breakdown.

The one line version If you are outside the US or Canada, the choice is already made: ChatGPT is available in most countries today, while Muse is still rolling out country by country. If you are in the US or Canada, keep reading, because then it gets interesting.

1. Availability: the biggest difference

This is the comparison that matters most, and it is not close. ChatGPT is available in most countries right now. Muse launched in the US first, Canada followed weeks later, and everywhere else is still waiting. Meta is expanding, but there is no public date for most countries.

2. Getting in: sign up vs invite code

ChatGPT asks you to sign up and you are in. Muse runs on invite codes. That sounds like friction, but the code system pays you: according to Muse's current invite copy, the referrer receives 1 billion Muse tokens and the new user receives 1 billion Muse tokens when a code is redeemed. Redeem it in Settings within 48 hours of joining.

3. What they actually do all day

Here is the part most comparisons skip: for everyday work the two overlap heavily. Writing, answering questions, planning, summarizing, brainstorming, learning new topics. If your use case is one of these, either assistant gets the job done, and switching from one to the other takes an afternoon.

The honest differentiator is ecosystem, not raw ability. Muse is Meta's personal AI agent, built to live inside Meta's world. ChatGPT is a standalone product with its own apps and integrations. So the better question is rarely which is smarter. It is which one fits the apps and habits you already have.

Curious what people actually do with Muse once they are in? Browse how Muse helps creators grow for real workflows.

4. The decision, in three questions

  • Where do you live? Outside the US or Canada, use ChatGPT today and join the Muse waitlist for launch day. Inside, you have a real choice.
  • How do you want to get in? If you like the idea of starting with a token bonus on both sides, Muse's invite system is genuinely generous. If you want zero setup, ChatGPT's signup is simpler.
  • Which apps do you live in? Heavy Meta app user, lean Muse. Living in a different ecosystem, lean ChatGPT.

5. The case for trying both

Nothing forces a single choice. Many people keep ChatGPT for general use and add Muse the moment they can get in, then let a week of real usage decide. A free invite code makes the experiment cost nothing: you get the token bonus either way.
