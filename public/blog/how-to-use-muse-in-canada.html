How to Use Muse in Canada

Muse has worked in Canada since September 18, 2026, ten days after the US launch, and Canada was the second country in the world to get it. This guide covers how to get Muse on every device, why some Canadians still see a not available message, what it costs in Canada, the French question, and what Canada still does not have, including Voice and the Charm device.

Is Muse available in Canada?

Yes. Muse launched in Canada on September 18, 2026, ten days after the US launch on September 8. The announcement came from Muse's own account on X, which posted "Made it to Canada. Where to next?" at 3:12 p.m. Pacific time that day, pointing Canadians to muse.ai. iPhone in Canada reported the launch the same day. Meta's newsroom first named Canada on September 29, 2026, in its post on Muse for Small Business, which describes Muse as available in the US and Canada. The dated record is assembled in the California Today availability guide, which records each country with the Meta source that says so.

Canada's place in the rollout matters for expectations. It was the second market, ahead of Japan on October 23, Singapore on October 28, and Mexico on November 5, 2026. Muse is now live in five countries, and Canada is one of the two that has had it since September. The full list of countries where Muse is live tracks every launch. Canadians are not waiting for a launch. They are using a product that has been live for nearly two months, with a few features still missing, covered below.

One early hint is worth recording because it shows how the launch was telegraphed. A few days after the US launch, Meta's chief AI officer Alexandr Wang replied to a question about Canada in a way that suggested it was coming, and a week later the "Made it to Canada" post arrived. No country has been teased that way since. The launch itself, not the hint, is the fact that counts, and the launch date is September 18, 2026.

How to get Muse in Canada

On iPhone, open the App Store and search for "Muse from Meta." Check the seller name: Meta Platforms, Inc. Download it, sign in with your Meta account, and you are in. The App Store listing carries a 17+ age rating, and Muse itself requires users to be 18 or older. Canada had iPhone and web access on launch day, confirmed in the same day coverage.

On Android, open Google Play and search for the same listing. Launch coverage described Android for Canada as still to come at the time, with iOS and web confirmed first. Later trackers describe Canada as having the same apps as the US, but no separate Canada Android date was announced, so the honest instruction is to check Google Play on your own device. If the listing is there, Android has reached your account. If it is not, use the web version at muse.ai, which works in any browser.

On the web, go to muse.ai and sign in, or start at muse.ai/join, the open entry point that needs no invite code. The web version was confirmed for Canada at launch, and it is the fallback whenever an app store listing lags. On a Mac, download the app directly from Meta at ai.meta.com/muse/download, not from the Mac App Store. Meta released the Mac app about ten days after the US launch, and the Mac app is documented for the US and Canada. If you give it permission, it can work with your files and with Messages, Calendar, and Notes. The Mac details are covered in the tryagentsonic guide to Muse on iPad and Mac, published on October 7, 2026.

Inside WhatsApp, Muse works as a chat you message like a contact, after connecting your account the first time through an entry link from the Muse site or app. The WhatsApp surface follows the same country rule as everything else, and Canada is inside it.

On iPad, be careful with dates. The iPad version arrived on October 7, 2026, in an update to the Muse iOS app, and early reports described it as US only for now, with Canadians on iPad having to wait. No separate Canada iPad date has been verified in any source checked for this guide. Check the App Store listing on your own iPad before assuming it is there. The iPad question is the one device question in this guide with no confirmed Canadian answer, and this guide will not invent one.

Why some Canadians still see "not available"

Canada is launched, and yet some Canadians hit a wall. Every verified explanation for that wall comes back to the same set of checks, and none of them means the product has left Canada. Work through them in order before concluding anything is broken.

First, the store region. The region your App Store or Google Play shows is set in your Apple ID or Google account, not by your IP address. A proxy or VPN changes the address you appear from. It does not change the store region, and if your store region is set to a country where Muse has not launched, the app will not appear in your store at all. That store region mechanism is set out in the proxycove breakdown of how Meta decides your country. If the app is missing from your store, check the country on your Apple ID or Google account before anything else.

Second, the Meta account country. The country on your Meta account is the primary signal, set in your Meta account settings, and it does not move when you change phones. Your apparent network location is compared against it, and a mismatch can read as unavailable or try again later. A Canadian traveling outside the launched countries can hit exactly that mismatch: an account created in Canada, used from a network somewhere Muse is not available, with the two signals disagreeing. The account first picture is set out in the GetMuseHelp guide to the country block, last verified on November 8, 2026.

Third, stale guides. Storefront checks run before September 18 showed Canada empty, because Canada had not launched yet. Some guides published weeks after the launch still repeat those pre launch results. One guide to Muse Voice, published after the Canada launch, still states that Muse is not listed on Canada's App Store. That statement repeats a check from before the launch, and the live listing is the authority, not the article. Check what muse.ai and your own store show today, not what a month old blog says. The early check pattern is described in a November 10, 2026 account of the launch era checks, which notes that Apple's storefront data showed nothing in Canada among other stores at that time.

Fourth, surfaces ship on different schedules. The iPhone app, the Android app, the Mac app, the web, and the WhatsApp chat have not all opened in the same hour in every market. A block on one surface does not prove a block on all of them. If the app store fails you, try muse.ai in a browser before concluding your account is the problem.

If all four checks pass and the message persists, the message on muse.ai is the authority for your account on that day. Availability has changed without much notice throughout this rollout, and the live product outranks any guide, including this one.

Do you need an invite code in Canada?

No. Canadians do not need a code to use Muse. The open entry point at muse.ai/join needs no code, and signing up through the app needs none either. Download, sign in with your Meta account, and you are in. There is no waitlist inside Canada and no queue to join.

A code is still worth having, because a code is a reward, not a key. Redeem one within 48 hours of creating your account and both people get 1 billion Muse tokens: you, and the person who shared the code. One code per account, and codes are case sensitive, so copy the code exactly. The 48 hour window starts at account creation, not at first use, so redeeming early matters. Get a fresh muse referral code from the community pool, and read how to redeem a Muse invite code for the exact steps.

Codes do not clear geography. An invite code clears nothing for an account outside the launched countries, and it does not fix a store region problem. Inside Canada, a code is a token bonus. Outside the launched map, it is a souvenir. Do not pay for one: codes are free in the community pool.

What does Muse cost in Canada?

The plan structure is the same as in the US, and the currency is the open question. Muse is free with a weekly usage limit. Power costs $20 a month for 500 million Muse tokens a week. Maximum costs $100 a month for 3 billion Muse tokens a week. The paid plans buy usage capacity, not features: the agent, the connectors, and the approvals are the same on every tier. The free tier's limit is not published as a number by Meta; the up to 100 million tokens a week figure comes from Mark Zuckerberg's launch statement, attributed that way wherever it appears.

Here is the part no guide should state flatly. Meta's help page lists the prices with a dollar sign and names no currency, on a product sold in the US and Canada. That wording was read again on November 6, 2026 and found unchanged, and it is reported in the Plain AI Daily explainer. Whether Canadians see Canadian dollars at checkout, US dollars converted by their card, or a Canada specific price has not been verified in any source checked for this guide. Do not assume the $20 figure is $20 Canadian. Do not assume it is $20 US either. The checkout screen is the only figure that binds you.

Two more cautions belong here. Meta says benefits vary by region and account, and its subscriptions page describes Muse and its subscriptions as in limited testing and not available in all locations yet, so your upgrade screen may differ from another Canadian's. Confirm your own offer inside your own account, as the BenchLM comparison advises. And a payment card is reported to be required even to start on the free tier: TechCrunch reported it at launch, and later guides repeat it. Meta has not confirmed or denied it in the sources checked, so treat it as a report and budget for the step. The card report is noted in the BetterClaw comparison, last checked on November 10, 2026.

Muse is for adults 18 and over in Canada, matching the US rule. That 18 and older rule is Meta's own, stated in its product FAQ, while the App Store listing itself carries a 17+ age rating. The rule applies on every surface.

Does Muse work in French?

Canada is the one launched market where the language question is also a legal question, and the honest answer starts with what Meta has not published. Meta has not published a list of the languages the Muse agent supports, and its launch material is in English. That gap is noted in the DriveGood guide for Quebec dealers, published in October 2026, which advises testing any tool with real Quebec questions before letting it near buyers.

What Meta has done in French is communicate. Meta's own French language Connect newsroom post states that Muse is available in the United States, Canada, and Mexico, which shows Meta announcing Canada in French, not only in English. That post is noted in the AI Agents Library Charm availability article. A French announcement is not a French language support list, and the two should not be merged. The first proves Meta writes about Canada in French. The second would prove the agent handles French, and no such list exists.

In Quebec the stakes are higher than elsewhere in Canada. Under section 5 of the Charter of the French language, consumers of goods and services have the right to be informed and served in French. The Quebec government sums up the business side as respecting that right and making catalogues, advertising, and websites available in French. A business in Quebec that points Muse at buyers should test it with real Quebec French, including the phrasing buyers actually use, before trusting it with a customer conversation. DriveGood's advice is the right default: test first, deploy second, and treat untested language handling as unproven.

For personal use, the practical step is the same, minus the legal weight. Try Muse in French on a low stakes job, read what it produces, and judge the quality yourself. No guide can grade it for you, because no published test of Muse in French exists in the sources checked. Your own trial is the evidence.

Muse Mail: Canada included from day one

Muse Mail is the feature where Canada was treated as a first class market. On November 10, 2026, Muse got its own email address, an @muse.ai handle your agent can send and receive from, and the rollout covered paid subscribers in the US and Canada together. You can forward material to Muse or add it to an email thread. Paid Canadian subscribers can claim a handle now.

Free plans are next, with no date. On November 11, 2026, Alexandr Wang posted that Muse Mail will be out to everyone soon, including free plans, and urged paid subscribers to reserve their handle while they can. Wang gave no date for the free rollout, and he did not say whether the expansion will go beyond the US and Canada. The rollout and the free plan promise are covered in the allthingsmuse report on Muse Mail.

The setup takes a few minutes once the feature reaches your account, and the handle question is the one to act on: handles are claimed, and paid subscribers were urged to claim early ahead of the wider launch. If you are on a free plan, there is nothing to claim yet, and no date to wait for beyond "soon."

What Canada does not have yet

Voice is the biggest gap. Muse Voice, the spoken conversation mode with an expressive avatar, rolled out to all users in the US on November 5, 2026, on mobile, the web, and Mac. It is US only to start, and Meta has given no date for Canada. Canadian coverage has noted the gap directly: voice and avatars arrived in the US, not yet in Canada, and the November 5 post limits the rollout to the US without saying when Canadian users will get it. The gap is covered in the Canada report on Muse Voice and in the Muse Voice guide. If you are in Canada and the phone icon does not appear, nothing is broken on your side.

Outbound calling is the second gap, and it is narrower. On November 12, 2026, Muse gained the ability to phone businesses on your behalf, booking tables, making appointments, and waiting on hold, but only for paid subscribers in the US, and only to US businesses. Canadian subscribers, free or paid, do not have it, and Meta has not said which countries or plans come next. The launch is covered in the TechProlonged report, published on November 13, 2026.

The Charm device is the third gap, and it is a hardware gap. When Meta's storefronts were checked on October 12, 2026, the US product page offered an email notify form, while the Canadian, UK, Mexican, and Australian pages all returned "Not yet available in your region." Canada can use the Muse app today, and Canada's Muse Charm page still says the device is not available there. Software access does not imply hardware access: two separate rollouts, two separate schedules. The storefront check is documented in the AI Agents Library Charm article, and the device itself is covered in the Muse Charm guide. Meta has announced no Charm price and no firm on sale date anywhere.

Spaces is the exception that proves the pattern. Muse Spaces, announced on November 6, 2026, is available everywhere Muse is available, with no sharing at launch and no price stated. Canada has Spaces because Canada has Muse. The Spaces guide is at the Muse Spaces guide. When a feature is described as available everywhere Muse is, Canada is included. When a feature names the US only, Canada waits.

Muse for Small Business in Canada

Canada was named in the business offer from the start. Meta announced Muse for Small Business on September 29, 2026, describing Muse as a personal AI agent available in the US and Canada, and the announcement named the business connectors: Asana, Box, Canva, Dropbox, Figma, Granola, HighLevel, Intuit QuickBooks, Klaviyo, Lovable, Notion, Shopify, Slack, Stripe, and Zoom, plus Facebook Pages, Instagram professional analytics, and Meta ad accounts. The connector list and the announcement are covered in the Kolton guide to the Shopify integration.

The offer is not a separate app, a separate subscription, or a separate signup. It is a set of business skills and connectors inside the existing Muse app, at the same prices as consumer Muse, with the same approval rule: nothing publishes, sends, or spends without your approval. A Canadian owner operator can draft campaigns, read sales history, and flag odd expenses from the same account a consumer uses.

One caution applies to the connector list. An announcement is not the same as a live connector in your account. Rollout has been staged, published lists have disagreed with each other, and some announced partners were still not live weeks after being named. Check the connectors inside your own account before planning work around one, and keep the dated record in the Muse connectors list open while you do. Your account is the authority for your account.

One setting to change today

Canadian users can do this right now, because the account already exists. Open the Muse app, go to Settings, then Data controls, and turn off "Help improve our AI models." Toggle the switch off and confirm "Turn off" when the dialog asks. From that point, Meta may not use your chats to improve its models, and your chats stay out of training data.

There is no reason to wait. The switch governs chats from the start of the account, so every chat before you set it falls under the old rule. Setting it today covers everything from today forward, and it changes nothing about what the agent can do for you. It is a choice about your words, and it takes seconds.

Pair it with the approval habit. Approvals control what the agent does in the world. The data switch controls what Meta does with your chats. A Canadian account with both set is an account whose owner is actually in charge.

Get started in Canada

Setup for a new Canadian user takes under half an hour. Download "Muse from Meta" from the App Store or Google Play, or start at muse.ai/join in a browser, and sign in with your Meta account. Turn off "Help improve our AI models" first. Connect one inbox and one calendar. Give the agent one small job, a single booking or a single draft, and read the approval before you grant it.

Redeem a code inside the 48 hour window. You do not need a code to use Muse, but redeeming one within 48 hours of creating your account earns both people 1 billion Muse tokens. The steps are in how to redeem a Muse invite code. One code per account, case sensitive, and the window starts at account creation.

Then check your own upgrade screen before paying for anything. The prices on Meta's help page name no currency, and benefits vary by region and account, so the screen inside your account is the only price that applies to you. Start free, use the weekly allowance, and upgrade only when you actually hit the limit. If you run a business, check the connectors inside your account next, and test French handling with real Quebec questions before pointing the agent at buyers.
