Turn an Instagram Recipe Reel into a Grocery List with Muse

Time saved ~30 minMuse apps used: InstagramNeeds Muse invite access

That 30 second pasta reel you're never going to rewatch? Muse turns it into a grocery list, skips what you already own, and can order the rest for delivery.

How it works

  1. Save the reel in Instagram and send the link to Muse: "turn this recipe into a grocery list."
  2. Muse extracts the ingredients from the caption, on screen text, and linked recipe, scaling quantities to your serving size.
  3. It subtracts your pantry staples: olive oil, salt, garlic, which it remembers from a one time setup.
  4. You get a clean list grouped by store section, with a one tap option to send it to a grocery delivery app.
  5. Confirm and order. Muse shows the cart and total before anything is purchased, you always approve.

The exact prompt to try

I saved this Instagram reel, it's a creamy garlic pasta recipe for 2. Turn it into a grocery list for me. Skip anything I'd already have as a pantry staple (I keep olive oil, garlic, salt, pepper, and dried pasta). Group the rest by store section, and tell me the estimated total if I ordered it all for delivery.

Where you'll hit approval gates

Reality check: Muse can't watch video like a human, it works from the reel's caption, overlaid text, and any linked recipe. Reels with no written recipe get estimated ingredients, which can be off. And the grocery order always pauses for your confirmation: nothing is bought without you tapping approve.

Why it beats the old way

  • No more pausing and rewinding a 30 second reel to scribble ingredients
  • Quantities scaled to your household automatically
  • Pantry staples remembered once, skipped forever
  • List grouped by aisle, one trip, no backtracking

Frequently asked

Can Muse watch an Instagram recipe reel?

You save the reel and forward the link to Muse. It reads the caption, on screen text, and recipe card to extract ingredients and steps.

Can Muse order groceries automatically?

It prepares the cart and shows you the total, you confirm before any order is placed. Nothing is purchased without your approval.

Does it know what I already have at home?

If you tell Muse your pantry staples once, it remembers them and skips those items on every future grocery list.

What if the reel has no written recipe?

Muse can still estimate ingredients from the visuals and audio, but accuracy drops. Best results come from reels with captions or a linked recipe.
