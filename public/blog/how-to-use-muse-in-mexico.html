How to Use Meta Muse in Mexico

Muse is available in Mexico. Install the official app on iPhone or Android, or open muse.ai on the web. No VPN, no store tricks, no workarounds. This guide covers setup, the invite code that earns you free tokens, and the one privacy setting to check first.

Yes, Mexico is supported. Meta's Connect 2026 materials list Mexico alongside the US and Canada.

1. Get Muse from the official place

Muse launched on September 8, 2026, on iPhone, Android, and muse.ai. You do not need a country trick to start in Mexico. Use the official app or website, create your account, then redeem a code if you want free Muse tokens.

Choose the route that fits the device you already use.

On iPhone

Open the official Muse app in the App Store. Install it, open it, and follow the sign up steps on screen.

On Android

Open the Google Play Store on your phone. Find the official Muse app from Meta. Install it, open it, and follow the sign up steps on screen.

On the web

Open muse.ai in your browser. Follow the sign up steps there. This is useful if you want to try Muse before you install an app.

Need an invite code? Get one from the Built with Muse community pool.

2. Sign up first, then redeem your code

You do not need an invite code to join Muse in Mexico. The code is for a free token reward. When a new person joins with a code and redeems it in Settings within 48 hours, both people get 1 billion Muse tokens.

  1. Open Muse and finish the sign up steps.
  2. Open Settings after your account is ready.
  3. Find the invite code area.
  4. Enter your code and complete the redemption.
  5. Do this within 48 hours of joining so both people can get the reward.

If one code does not work, return to the community pool and try a different available code. Do not wait until the 48 hour window is almost over.

3. Use your first hour well

You will learn more by finishing one useful task than by testing random prompts. Start small and use something from your real life.

  • Check your privacy setting. Open Settings and turn off Help improve our AI models if you do not want your data shared to help improve the models. This is a privacy caution. It does not hide your location or change country access.
  • Give Muse one clear task. Say what you want, what matters, and what it must ask before doing.
  • Check the result. Fix anything that is wrong before you use or share it.
  • Save the method that worked. You can use it again instead of starting from zero.

Need an idea for your first useful task? Browse Muse workflows made by the community. Pick one that matches a real job you need to finish.

4. Share what works after you have tried it

Muse is new and there are no big names yet. Share your workflows and become the person people follow. Show a clear result and explain what another person can copy.

5. If the app does not appear yet

The rollout may not reach every Mexican App Store listing at the same moment. If the official app is missing, wait for the rollout and check again later. Do not change your store country, use a VPN, or install a file from an unknown website.

You can also try muse.ai while you wait. If the website does not open for your account, wait and try again later. A missing app or blocked account does not prove that Mexico is unsupported.

Get started

Frequently asked questions

Is Meta Muse available in Mexico?
Yes. Meta's Connect 2026 materials list Mexico alongside the US and Canada. Access can still take time to appear for every account and store listing.
Do I need an invite code to join?
No. You can join without a code. A code gives free Muse tokens when you redeem it in Settings within 48 hours of joining.
What do both people get from a code?
Both people get 1 billion Muse tokens when the new person redeems the code in time. This is based on the invite copy shown by Meta.
What should I do if the app is missing?
Wait for the Mexican store rollout and check again later. You can try muse.ai in the meantime. Do not use an unofficial download or a country workaround.
