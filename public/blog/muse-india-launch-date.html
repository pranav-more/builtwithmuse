2. What is actually known

Muse is rolling out through an invite only system, and the rollout started in the US. That is the confirmed picture. New countries open up as Meta expands the rollout, and India is one of the countries still waiting.

Meta shares rollout news on their official channels. Until you see it there, nothing else counts as news, no matter how confident the post sounds.

Until then, the invite system is the whole story. Codes are how people get in during the rollout, and the community pool exists so nobody has to wait on knowing the right person.

3. Which rumors to ignore

Fake launch dates spread fast because demand is high. Ignore screenshots of dates with no source, countdown pages from strangers, and anyone selling early access. None of them know more than you do.

A useful test: if the claim does not link to a Meta announcement, it is not a launch date. It is content made to get your click.
