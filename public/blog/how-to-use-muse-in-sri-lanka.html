Is Muse available in Sri Lanka?

No. As of November 13, 2026, Muse is live in five countries: the US, Canada, Japan, Singapore, and Mexico. Sri Lanka is not among them, and Meta has announced no launch date for Sri Lanka or for any other country in South Asia. The country list shows the same five markets, and nothing else.

There is no Sri Lanka specific announcement to parse. No Sri Lankan outlet has reported a launch date, a statement from Meta, or a local launch event, and Meta publishes no country list or expansion timeline. Its help pages say only that Muse is not available in all locations yet. An availability tracker checked against Meta's own announcements lists every unlaunched country as no launch announced.

The position is the same across the region. India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nepal, and Sri Lanka are all unlaunched, and none has a date. For Sri Lankan readers, that means the honest answer is short: not available, no date, and no reliable way to predict one.

This guide will be updated when Meta announces a Sri Lanka launch. Until then, the answer stays the same, and any page that gives you a date is guessing. The country list on this site tracks every launch as it happens, and it is the fastest way to check whether anything has changed.

What does the India news mean for Sri Lanka?

The one piece of regional news concerns India, not Sri Lanka. On November 9, 2026, The Hindu reported that Meta had set rupee pricing for Muse ahead of an India launch, Power at ₹1,799 a month and Maximum at ₹8,999 a month, with marginally higher charges through Google Play and Apple billing. The report said it was unclear whether the prices were final, and Meta did not respond to the paper's queries.

Even if India launches, it does not imply Sri Lanka follows. The five launches show no regional pattern: Japan and Singapore are neighbours in Asia, but they arrived five days apart through separate announcements, and Mexico came a week later with no hint of the rest of Latin America. Each market is announced on its own day, with its own store listing.

The India report is still worth watching, because it is the only South Asia signal of any kind. If Meta confirms an India launch, it will show the company is willing to launch in the region and will give Sri Lankan readers a real precedent for pricing, language support, and connectors. Until Meta confirms it, the report is a rumor with a byline, not a date.

What the store checks show

Storefront checks are the most direct evidence of where Muse is. A sweep of 32 App Stores on November 7, 2026 found the app in five: the US, Canada, Japan, Singapore, and Mexico. Sri Lanka was not among them, and the app is not listed in the Sri Lankan store.

An earlier check tells the same story. On September 10, 2026, a storefront check found the app in the US store and in none of 15 other storefronts checked, including India, Japan, Singapore, and Mexico, all of which launched later. Two months later the list had grown to five, and Sri Lanka was still not on it.

Storefront checks matter because they cannot be faked by rumor pages. An app is either listed in a country's store or it is not, and the answer is the same for everyone who checks. When Sri Lanka's store lists the app, the launch is real. Until then, it is not.

You can repeat the check yourself in under a minute. Open your phone's app store, search for Muse from Meta, and look for the listing. If it is absent, the launch has not happened. The same check works in the Play Store. It is faster than reading rumor pages, and it cannot be wrong, because the store is the launch.

Will Muse work in Sinhala and Tamil?

English is certain. Muse's interface and help pages are in English, and the free tier runs in English everywhere it is available. Sri Lankan readers who work in English will have no language barrier on launch day.

Sinhala and Tamil are unconfirmed. Meta has published no language list for Muse, and no source has reported Sinhala or Tamil support. The only language claim with a source is Spanish: the App Store listing includes Spanish among 39 languages, and Mexico launched in Spanish. That tells you Meta ships Muse in more than English. It tells you nothing about Sinhala or Tamil.

The practical advice is to test on launch day with a low stakes job in the language you actually use, and judge the output. Language support for a new market is often announced at launch, and if Sinhala or Tamil support exists, it will be in the launch announcement, not in a rumor page.

What about Muse in WhatsApp?

Muse inside WhatsApp is closed in Sri Lanka, because it follows the same country list as the app. The gate is the Meta account, so the surface you open does not matter. WhatsApp is widely used in Sri Lanka, which makes the distinction between Muse and Meta AI important to get right.

Meta AI, the assistant already inside WhatsApp, is a different product. It answers questions in chat. It does not connect to your inbox and calendar, complete errands, or ask for approval before spending or sending. The comparison guide explains the difference, and the WhatsApp guide explains the Muse chat route for when it arrives.

No Sri Lanka date exists for the WhatsApp route either. Mexico's launch coverage said WhatsApp use would follow, without a date, which suggests the chat surface can lag the app. Sri Lanka has neither yet, and any account offering Muse over WhatsApp today is unofficial.

Can a VPN unlock Muse in Sri Lanka?

No verified method exists. The check runs at sign in, against your Meta account, and compares your account country, store region, and payment card country. A VPN changes only the network address you appear from, so it cannot change the signals that matter.

The terms question is separate and clearer. Meta's terms prohibit misrepresenting your region, and an availability guide that reviewed the question in November 2026 said some sources advise against VPN workarounds entirely. The risk is not theoretical: an enforcement action against a Meta account affects Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp together.

Sri Lanka's position makes the wait easier than the workaround. Singapore, a nearby market, launched on October 28, which shows Meta is expanding in Asia. That is not a promise for Sri Lanka, but it is a better reason for patience than any tutorial promising access today.

What would Muse cost in Sri Lanka?

The only pricing figures in circulation are American, and they are reported rather than published: a free tier with a weekly usage limit, Power at $20 a month, and Maximum at $100 a month. Paid tiers buy weekly usage capacity, not features. Sri Lanka has no counterpart to any of them, and Meta has announced nothing about Sri Lanka pricing.

The India report, if it proves true, would give the region its first local pricing reference: Power at ₹1,799 a month and Maximum at ₹8,999 a month, as reported by The Hindu on November 9, 2026. Meta has not confirmed those figures, and even confirmed India pricing would not be Sri Lanka pricing. The report itself said it was unclear whether the prices were final.

Do not convert US dollars into rupees and call the result the price. No rupee price has been announced, and local pricing depends on app store rules, taxes, and Meta's own decisions for the market. The checkout screen in your own account, on launch day, is the only figure that counts.

The data setting to change first

Before you connect an inbox, a calendar, or anything else, change one setting. The path is Settings, then Data controls, then the toggle labelled "Help improve our AI models". Turn it off and confirm "Turn off". The effect is immediate: Meta may not use your chats to improve its models from that point on.

The reason to do it first is timing: the setting covers chats from the moment you change it, and nothing before it. The first session is when most people test the agent with real, personal material, because that is how you learn what it can do. Set the switch before that session, and the testing is covered.

Nothing else changes. Connectors still connect, the agent still asks for approval before important actions, and tasks still complete. The switch governs only what Meta may do with your words afterwards, which is exactly why it deserves the first minute of launch day.

What does the Singapore launch mean for Sri Lanka?

Singapore launched on October 28, 2026, as the fourth market, five days after Japan. It is the closest launched market to Sri Lanka, and the only one in Asia besides Japan. Its launch shows Meta is willing to expand in Asia, and it shows an English language market can launch without the language work a Spanish or Japanese launch needs.

But Singapore is a poor predictor for Sri Lanka. It is a regional hub with its own connector ecosystem, its own app store dynamics, and a launch announced through Meta's regional channels. None of that transfers to Sri Lanka, and no announcement has linked the two markets. Proximity on a map is not proximity in a rollout plan.

The useful precedent from Singapore is the pattern, not the place. It arrived through a regional announcement, with a store listing the same day, and no advance date. A Sri Lanka launch will most likely look the same: announced the day it happens, visible in the store the same day, and covered by local press within hours. New countries arrive through regional announcements first.

Until then, the Singapore launch is a reason for patience, not for expectation. Asia is on the rollout map, and Sri Lanka is in Asia, but the map has no dates on it. Watch for a Sri Lanka announcement, not for another country's launch.
